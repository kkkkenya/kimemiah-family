# Features

Eight sections. The five used most sit on a bottom bar on the phone (Home, Stock and Shopping, Jobs, Meals, Money). Budget, Meeting and Contacts sit under a "More" tab.

## 1. Home

A quick look at the house:

- Items low or out of stock
- Open jobs, and jobs marked "Needs a volunteer"
- Dad's money left this month
- Date of the next family meeting
- Today's meals and who's cooking
- Latest thank-yous

## 2. Stock and Shopping

**Stock**
- Each item has: name, category, unit, Have, Minimum, usual price (KES), usual shop.
- One tap on **+** or **−** changes Have.
- Status shows by colour: OK, Low (at or below Minimum), Out (zero).
- Categories: Kitchen staples, Fresh produce, Drinks and breakfast, Cleaning, Bathroom and personal, Utilities.
- Add, edit or remove items at any time.

**Shopping list**
- Low and Out items appear on their own, with a suggested quantity to buy.
- Anyone can add a one-off item, for example "birthday cake for Mom".
- Grouped by shop: Supermarket, Market, Mama mboga, Wholesaler, Other.
- Estimated total in KES.
- **"I'm going to [shop]"** button: tells the others someone is heading out, so they can add requests first and only one person makes the trip.
- Tick items while shopping. **"Put away"** at home adds bought quantities back into stock and offers to log the spend in Dad's money.

## 3. Jobs board

- Anyone posts a job: title, optional notes, optional due date, optional repeat (daily, weekly, monthly).
- Anyone taps **"I'll do it"**, then **"Done"**.
- A person can drop a job they picked up, so it goes back on the board.
- Repeating jobs come back on their own after being done.
- A job nobody has taken for **3 days** gets a **"Needs a volunteer"** flag and goes onto the next meeting agenda.
- **Help requests**: a quick post such as "Can someone help me carry shopping at 4pm?"
- **Thank-you wall**: when a job is done, others can leave a short thank-you. No points, no ranking.

Starter jobs to suggest (the family edits these at the first meeting):

## 4. Dad's money

- **Money in**: date, amount, how it came (M-Pesa, bank, cash), note.
- **Money out**: date, amount, who spent it, shop, what for, category.
- Balance left, updated on its own.
- Optional photo of the receipt (later phase).
- **Summary for Dad**: one button builds a short monthly summary (received, spent by category, balance) to copy into WhatsApp.

## 5. Monthly budget

- A budget per category: Food staples, Fresh produce/market, Cleaning, Personal care, Utilities, Other.
- Compares budget with actual spend from Dad's money.
- Shows what the current shopping list would cost against what's left.

## 6. Family meeting

- Next meeting date and this meeting's host (the host rotates).
- Agenda with timings. "Needs a volunteer" jobs and help requests are added to it on their own.
- Space for notes during the meeting.
- **Decisions log**: each decision has a date and a short line. One tap turns a decision into a job.
- Ground rules shown at the top.

See [03-family-meeting.md](03-family-meeting.md) for the first meeting.

## 7. Weekly meal plan

- A week grid: breakfast, lunch and supper for each day. Lunch can be marked "everyone out" on weekdays.
- **Saved meals** with their ingredients, for example:
  - Ugali and sukuma: unga, sukuma wiki, onions, tomatoes, cooking oil
  - Githeri: maize, beans, onions, tomatoes, Royco
  - Chapati and ndengu: wheat flour, cooking oil, green grams, onions, dhania
  - Pilau: rice, beef, pilau masala, onions, tomatoes, cooking oil
- Pick a saved meal for a slot, or type a new one.
- **"Add ingredients to shopping list"**: anything the week's meals need that stock doesn't cover goes on the list, with the meal named beside it.
- **Cooking is volunteered too.** Each meal slot has an "I'll cook" button, the same way jobs work. A cooked meal can get a thank-you.
- Copy last week's plan to start a new week quickly.

## 8. Household contacts

- One shared phone book for the house: fundi, plumber, electrician, landlord or caretaker, gas delivery, water vendor, garbage collection, mama mboga, nearest clinic.
- Each contact has a name, role, phone number, area and a short note ("charges KES 500 call-out", "delivers Tue and Fri").
- A copy button for each number. Tap-to-call may not work inside the app, so the number is always shown as plain text too.
- Emergency numbers pinned at the top.
