# Features

The bottom bar has five tabs: **Home · Stock · Shop · Jobs · Money**. Contacts, Meeting and Settings are buttons at the top right of every screen, next to a sync dot (green: synced; amber with a count: saved on the phone, syncs when there's signal). There is no Meals section: cooking for the house is a job like any other. Lists use compact cards (name and tag, one status line, one button); tapping a card opens its details in a sheet from the bottom.

Rules that apply everywhere:

- **Every tap saves on the phone first** and syncs when there's signal. The phone shows "Saved on this phone · will sync", then "Synced".
- **Undo, not "Are you sure?".** A tap shows a short Undo bar, for example "Cooking oil −1 · Undo".
- **Works offline.** Everything can be read and changed without signal, except joining or leaving a job. That needs signal so two people don't take the same share.
- **Every action is credited automatically.** Each phone is set to one person ("Who are you?") at setup.
- **Fair, not ranked.** Jobs show who is on them. Home shows each person's time given to the house over 2 weeks against an equal share, in family order and never ranked (see Jobs). Spending is never shown per person. Thank-yous go only to the person thanked.
- Money shows as KES 1,250. Dates show as Tue 21 Oct. Times are Nairobi time.

## 1. Home ("Today")

Top to bottom (cards appear only when there's something to show):

- "Habari, Greg", your picture, and **tonight's supper headcount** ("🏠 4/5 home for supper (Vinnie out)") with "I'll be out tonight" (resets each day).
- For Peter, who lives away: money runs coming up and bills to pay, first.
- Jobs that need rescuing and "Still yours" reminders; urgent jobs ("Water is back!") with **I'm on it**; who's out shopping right now.
- **Your jobs** with Done.
- One-tap strip: Water is back, Arrange kitchen, Wash dishes, Mark something out (Stock), Read a receipt, Say asante.
- **Essentials needed now**: essential items that are Out or Low, each with its Quickmart price, the total, and buttons to the cart.
- Can-wait upgrades on the list that are on sale this week.
- Jobs needing people (Light first for anyone with No heavy jobs on), the 2-week job picture, where everyone is, contact search, alerts, and your Home photo if you chose one.

## 2. Stock and Shop (two tabs)

**Stock tab**: everything in the house by category, + / − and OK / Low / Out (details below under Stock), plus:
- **Read a receipt**: a photo of a Naivas or Quickmart till receipt (read on the phone; the reader loads only when used) or pasted text. Each line is matched to a house item (you can change or skip it), packs are converted (3 L oil = 3 litres), then one tap adds it all to stock, saves the prices paid as usual prices and logs the receipt total as spending.
- **Tools & equipment**: panga, slasher, hedge shears, jembe, rake, hosepipe, dog brush, extension cable, ladder. Where each one is (Store room, Back verandah, Dog kennel, Main house), working or needs fixing (with "Post a repair job"), and who used it last.
- **Likely running low?** when an item still marked OK has used 80% of its usual time between restocks: Still OK (asks again in 3 days) or Mark Low / Add to the list.

**Shop tab**: two views, **Catalog** (opens first) and **Cart / List**.

**Catalog**
- Every Naivas and Quickmart product the scraper finds (about 7,000 today, Quickmart only while Naivas blocks us), kept on the phone and searchable with no signal.
- Search by name, brand or size. Aisles: On sale, House usuals, Unga rice and oil, Dairy bread and eggs, Liquor, Tea coffee and drinks, Cleaning, Toiletries, Home and upgrades, Market and mama mboga (house items, since market prices aren't online).
- Sort by house usuals, best value per kg / litre / piece, biggest discount, lowest price, or A to Z. Filter by store deals, where it's cheaper, or tier.
- Each card shows the Quickmart price (and price per kg / litre / piece). Cards group every brand of the same pack; "Show all brands" lists them one by one. If Naivas prices ever come through, both show side by side with the cheaper one marked.
- **+ Add** puts it in the family cart with its tier (the house item's tier, else the catalog's), then shows Essential / Full shop / Upgrade and a − 1 + stepper right on the card.
- A cart bar stays at the bottom: items, the total at each store, and "View cart".

**Stock**
- Packaged items (unga, oil, sugar, soap) use **+ / −** counts.
- Fresh produce (sukuma, tomatoes, onions) uses one tap: **OK / Low / Out**. Nobody counts tomatoes.
- Each item has a category, unit, minimum, usual price (KES) and usual shop.
- Status shows by icon, word and colour together: OK, Low, Out.
- Each item keeps a short history of who changed it and when.

**Shopping list**
- Low and Out items appear on their own, grouped by shop: **Naivas, Quickmart, Supermarket (either), Market, Mama mboga, Wholesaler**, plus Gas dealer, Water vendor and M-Pesa for things no supermarket sells.
- **No kiosk.** Kiosk prices run 20–40% above the supermarket, so milk, bread, eggs, sugar and tissue belong to the supermarket run.
- Anyone can add a one-off item and pick how urgent it is in one tap: **Essential**, **Full shop** or **Can wait**.
- Tick items as you shop; mark "Not found" if the shop is out.
- **✅ Shopping done · add all to stock (KES X)** adds what you bought to stock, logs the spend at the shop prices (one tap fixes a price), clears the basket and marks the supermarket run done on Jobs. If nothing was ticked, it offers the whole list to untick what you didn't buy. Catalog items not on the house list can be tracked at home from there.
- **Start shopping in store**: the screen stays on, tick targets get bigger, and a bar shows the trolley total, what's left on the list and money left. "Shelf empty? Swap brand" offers the next cheapest of the same pack.
- **Plan a supermarket run** posts the 2-person money run on Jobs with the cart's total, which follows the cart until the run.

**Three modes** (top of the list, each with its own estimated total):

| Mode | What's in it | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Essentials** | Unga, rice, oil, sugar, milk, bread, salt, eggs, tissue, soap, gas | Mid-month top-ups, tight weeks |
| **Full shop** | Essentials plus tea, Royco, spices, margarine, washing powder, dish soap, bleach, drinks | The bi-weekly or monthly bulk run |
| **+ Upgrades** | Can-wait items: broom, mop, bucket, basin, containers, pegs, bulbs, cloths, mats | When there's room in the budget, or one is on a deep deal |

Below the modes: "Estimated: KES 3,450 · Money left this month: KES 6,200", in amber when the list costs more than what's left.

**Anti-kiosk trips**
- **"I'm going to Naivas / Quickmart"** first checks the fast movers (milk, bread, eggs, tissue, sugar, unga). Anything Low, or not checked in 5 days, is offered for the trip list: "Keep them on your trip list so we skip the kiosk this week?"
- During the trip, a **"Grab while you're there (running low)"** card stays at the top.
- Everyone else sees "Mom is going to Quickmart. Add anything you need." (the push alert for this comes with Phase 7).
- If a bulk item runs out more than 4 days before the next big run, twice in a row, it gets a quiet note: "Ran out early last 2 runs. Consider buying more."

**Supermarket prices** (updated every Friday, all usable offline)
- Each item shows the **best brand and store**: "Best: Fresh Fri 2l · KES 545 at Naivas (sale · save KES 85)", with the other store's price underneath.
- **Brand rules per item**: any brand (cheapest), prefer a brand (with a note when a rival is 12%+ off), or only that brand.
- **Compare brands and sizes**: a drawer listing every brand and pack at both stores, with the price per kg, litre or piece, so an empty shelf isn't a problem.
- **Bulk value** tip when a bigger pack is at least 8% cheaper per kg or litre.
- **Supermarket basket**: Naivas total vs Quickmart total for the current mode, deals and savings, and a "split the run" option when buying some items at each store saves KES 300 or more.
- **Tracked staples on offer this week**: items fine at home but 15%+ off, to restock early.
- **Find more in the catalog** from the list opens the Catalog view, which works offline.

Naivas blocks automated price tools, so the app shows **Quickmart prices only**. On a real Naivas receipt (21 Oct 2026), 9 of 13 shelf prices matched Quickmart, so they work as a guide for either store. Naivas stays a shop you can go to, list items for and log spending at. Market items (eggs at KES 470 a tray), the vet and Kenchic use the family's usual prices.

## 3. Jobs (volunteering)

- **Anyone can post a job.** One-tap chips for the usual ones (Water is back: fill tanks, Arrange kitchen, Wash dishes, Sweep the drive, Wash the dog, Plan a supermarket run), or **+ Add a job** for anything else.
- **Size = how long × how many people.** A job says how long it takes and how many people it needs, and the app shows its size from the two together: 20 min × 1 is a Quick job, 1 h × 2 people is a Medium job (2 h of work), 2 h × 2 people or more is a Big job. A separate tag says how physically hard it is: 🌿 Light, ⚡ Medium or 🏋️ Heavy, so Wanini can filter to Light jobs.
- **Team-ups with shares.** "I'll do it" / "Join in" picks a share (20% to 100%), each shown as time: 40% of a 1 h, 2-person job is about 48 min. A job is taken when it's 100% covered by enough people; one person can't fill a 2-person job alone. Optional "my part" notes ("Hold and soap", "Fetch water and rinse"). Joining needs signal, so two people can't take the same share.
- **Two-stage kitchen.** "Clean counters and arrange dishes" (Light) creates "Wash the arranged dishes" when it's done.
- **Money jobs** (supermarket and market runs, gas): two people go, planned 2–4 days ahead. They wait under **Upcoming & money** until someone taps "Money is ready" and the day comes.
- **Bills Peter pays** (the water bill): "Send to Peter on WhatsApp" with the details, then "Peter paid".
- **Reminders.** Every 2 hours (07:00–21:00) a job you're on shows "Still yours" on Home and Jobs, and as a push alert if alerts are on, until you tap Done. They pause while you're Out or Away.
- **Presence.** Switching to Out or Away while you're on a job offers to hand it to whoever's at home. Rescue cards and "Leave" say who's at home now. Leave asks first.
- **Stock makes jobs.** Gas marked Out posts "Refill the cooking gas cylinder" (money run, 2 people, urgent). Dog unga Low posts a vet run. Only one open job per item.
- **Weekly sign-up grid** (Meeting screen): who does dishes, cooks supper and feeds the dog on each day. Each day those jobs appear already taken by whoever signed up (or open, with a note, if they're away), with the usual reminders and rescue.
- **Real times.** "I'm starting" then Done records how long a job really took. After 2 runs, a usual job posts with its real time (the middle of the last 5) and the form shows "Usually takes 1 h 20 min".
- **Waiting jobs.** A job nobody takes for 3 days shows "Waiting 3 days · on the meeting agenda" and is listed on the Meeting screen. About the job, never about people.
- **Overdue rescue.** A taken job past its due time goes to the top with "Meant to be done by Vinnie, but it wasn't finished in time." Anyone can **Help them finish** (pick a share; the others' shares shrink to fit) or **Take it over** (the earlier people step back).
- Lists: **Needs people · In progress · Upcoming & money · Done & Asante**, plus **Light only** and **Team-ups** filters. "At home now" shows who's around to team up with.
- Repeating jobs come back on their own when done. **Asante** thanks each person who did a job.
- **Tools**: jobs that use tools (fence, slashing, dog wash, sweeping, tanks) show where the tools are, and Done asks where they went back and whether anything needs fixing (which posts a repair job).
- **Cook supper** shows how many are eating tonight.
- **How the work is shared.** Home shows each person's time given to the house in the last 14 days against an equal share (the house's total over the people at home; Peter, who lives away, and anyone set to Away aren't compared). Nothing is judged until the house has finished about 2 hours of jobs. Labels: Room to help (under half a share), Doing your part, Doing a lot (over 1.6 shares). Cooking for the house counts like any job.
- **Suggestions, not assignments.** Each open job shows "Suggested: Daniel": whoever at home has given least lately and the job suits (not heavy work for someone with No heavy jobs on, not someone taking it easy). That person gets an alert. Joining, or not, stays voluntary.
- **Choice to ease off.** Someone doing a lot is offered "Take it easy for a week" on Home and by alert at most once a week; no jobs are suggested to them until it ends. Someone with room to help gets, at most once a week, an alert listing jobs that suit them.

## 4. Money (house money, projects and budget)

- **This month**: money in, spent and left (‹ › for other months). Money set aside for projects isn't counted here.
- **+ Money in / − Money out**: amount, day, what for (a budget kind, or a project), how (M-Pesa, cash, bank), from / paid to, note. Put away and receipts log spending on their own.
- **📲 Paste an M-Pesa SMS** and the entry fills itself in: amount, who, day, which way, and the M-Pesa code. The same message can't be logged twice. A cash withdrawal isn't spending, so it isn't logged.
- **Money in unlocks runs**: when money comes in and what's left this month covers a planned supermarket, market or gas run, it flips to "Money ready" (soonest first, never promising the same money twice) and the people going get an alert.
- **Projects**: savings goals for **house work** (e.g. "Repaint the gate, KES 6,000") and **upgrades** (from the cart: "Save up for the upgrades"). Anyone adds money toward one and logs what's spent on it; a progress bar shows how close it is. A house-work project can post its job on Jobs, which unlocks ("Money ready") once the project reaches its target.
- **Entries can't be edited or deleted.** "Wrong? Correct it" adds a correcting entry; both then drop out of every total.
- **Budget**: an amount per kind each month (Food, Market and fresh, Cleaning, Personal care, Utilities, Dog, House projects, Upgrades, Other) against what was spent, red when over.
- **Budget rollover**: a plan repeats each month until changed. A kind can carry its leftovers (or overspend) into the next month. "Save up for a yearly cost" (school fees, insurance) makes a kind that puts aside a twelfth each month.
- **Bills**: water, cooking gas, internet and any others the family adds. Each payment says how much was bought (litres, kg, GB), so Money shows cost per unit, how long one lasts, use per day, and when a month runs heavier than usual. A bill nearly due posts a job ("Buy cooking gas") that links back to logging the payment. Home shows bills coming up and a gentle note when one runs 25% above usual, about the house and never a person.
- **Cash flow**: money in against money spent for six months, and what was kept this month.
- **Goals with a pace**: a project can have a date; the app says how much to put aside each month and whether it's on track.
- **Ask at the meeting**: any money entry or job can be put on the next family meeting's agenda as a question.
- **Money, Monarch style** (Money, Jobs and Home's money cards; spec in docs/monarch-mvp.html): on a phone Money is five short views (Overview, Budget, Entries, Bills, Goals); on a laptop, for projecting at the family meeting, everything shows at once with full tables (budget with carried-in and month-end pace, every entry with who, how and M-Pesa code, cash flow numbers, money in by person).
- **Flex budgeting**: each kind is Fixed (Internet), Flexible or Non-monthly; Overview shows flexible money left and about how much a day. From the 10th, flexible kinds show their month-end pace. A kind at 80% and at 100% sends one alert each month to the people at home.
- **Questions on an entry**: "Ask someone" sends a question about a payment to one person (push), who marks it "Looks right". Entries can be searched by payee, note, M-Pesa code or amount, and filtered.
- **M-Pesa rules**: after saving a new payee, the app offers to remember it ("NAIROBI WATER → Water bill"); next time a pasted message sorts itself.
- **Bills calendar and reminders**: Bills shows as a list or a month calendar (orange for due, green for paid; on a laptop, both side by side with bill names in the days). Each bill can remind everyone the evening before it's due (from 18:00, once per due date).
- **Spotting repeat payments**: a payee paid in 3 of the last 4 months on about the same day, and not already a bill, gets "Make it a bill?". Yes makes a monthly bill and a rule for that payee; "Not a bill" hides it on that phone.
- **Goals**: each goal has an emoji or a photo, an optional planned amount a month, and "Chipped in" with the names of everyone who gave, in the order they first gave (never by amount). When a goal is done, Home shows a thank-you to them for 3 days.
- **Settle up**: money out says whether it came from house money or the person's own money. Own-money spends add to what the house owes that person; "Pay back" logs the payback, which isn't counted as new spending.
- **Reports** (Money → Reports): a Sankey of where the money went for a month, 3 or 6 months (who put money in → Fixed / Flexible / Non-monthly / Kept → each kind; spending beyond what came in shows as "Earlier months"); each kind over six months (bars on a phone, a table of numbers on a laptop); spending by shop; and what the bills bought (litres, kg) month by month.
- **Monthly review** (Money → Reports, or Home from the 1st to the 7th): up to six cards about a month (in and spent with who put money in, kept, biggest spending against plan, bills and usage against usual, goals that moved, open questions and paybacks). A phone swipes them one at a time; a laptop shows them all for the meeting. "Put on the meeting agenda" adds a 15-minute "Money review" item that links back to it. "Send to Peter" sends the same figures on WhatsApp, and Money's summary button now uses the review too.
- **Summary for Peter**: one tap opens WhatsApp to Peter with the month (in, spent by kind, left, projects).

## 5. Meeting (under More)

- **Next meeting**: day and host. "Plan the next meeting" picks next Sunday and the next host in family order; "We've had the meeting" saves it and plans the one two weeks later.
- **Agenda** with timings: "Use the usual agenda" (welcome and ground rules, appreciation round, jobs, what's working, decisions; about 40 minutes), your own items, and jobs nobody has taken for 3 days with one tap to add.
- **Notes** during the meeting.
- **Decisions**: a short line each. "Make it a job" puts it on the Jobs board.
- Ground rules, the weekly sign-up grid, the 2-week job picture, and past meetings with their notes and decisions.

See [03-family-meeting.md](03-family-meeting.md) for the first meeting.

## 6. Contacts (under More)

- The house phone book: fundi, plumber, electrician, landlord or caretaker, gas delivery, water vendor, garbage collection, mama mboga, nearest clinic.
- Name, what they do, phone, area and a note ("charges KES 500 call-out").
- **Call** (Kenyan numbers dial as +254) or **Copy number**. Edit and remove, with Undo.
- Emergency numbers first. Searchable by name, job, area or number, here and from Home.

## 7. Settings (under More)

- **You**: the name shown in the app, your picture (8 to choose from, also offered right after "Who's using this phone?"), and the photo on your own Home screen: none, the family photo, or one you upload (shrunk on the phone, hidden location details dropped).
- Alerts on this phone (on or off).
- **Your jobs**: "No heavy jobs" (heavy jobs aren't suggested to you) and "Take it easy for a week" (no suggestions until then). Wanini starts with No heavy jobs on.
- **The house**: days before a waiting job goes on the meeting agenda, pause all alerts on every phone, and the ground rules.
- Who's using this phone, sync status, signed-in phones (sign one out, or all after a PIN change).
- Still to come: light / dark / match the phone.

## Push alerts

Turn them on per phone (Home or Settings > Alerts on this phone). Built so far, for Jobs:

1. **Urgent jobs** ("Water is back! Fill the tanks"): everyone at home except whoever posted it, straight away.
2. **"You're covered"**: when the last share of a team job comes in, the people already on it hear who joined.
3. **Past its time**: the people on a job hear once when it passes its due time, before the house sees the rescue banner.
4. **"Still yours"**: every 2 hours while you're on a job, only while you're At home.
5. **Handover**: when someone leaves a job or hands it over, whoever's at home is asked to take it.
6. **Suggested for you**: a new job goes to whoever at home has given least lately.
7. **How you're doing** (at most once a week each): "jobs that suit you" for someone with room to help, "want to take it easy this week?" for someone doing a lot.

Still to come from the original three: "going to the market, add requests" and "asante" alerts.

Nothing is sent between 21:00 and 07:00 or about something older than an hour. Several at once become one alert. Alerts never show money amounts. Each phone can turn alerts off, and the kill switch in settings stops them all. Jobs nobody took don't nag anyone; they go to the meeting agenda instead.

On Android, alerts work in Chrome. **On iPhone, alerts only work on iOS 16.4 or newer, and only after adding the app to the home screen.** Without alerts, the app still works fully, with the same reminders shown inside the app.
