## Advocate Response to Strategist

**Their position:** Build all 8 sections at three depths (deep, standard, thin). Hold a short meeting before the build to gather inputs and launch the app at meeting 2. One push event at launch, behind a flag. Meal-to-shopping logic and copy-last-week wait until 1.1.

**My response, per tension:**

1. **Meet first, launch at meeting 2: Modify.** Strategist is right that the family should shape the app, but the user chose "everything at once", and that decision is theirs. My compromise is to use Strategist's own fallback: a private walkthrough with Mom plus a WhatsApp poll for shops, meals and contacts before the build. Meeting 1 is then the launch. If the user *does* want a pre-meeting, keep the gap to two weeks or less and show a clickable preview with seeded data, so people give feedback on something real rather than in the abstract. Whichever meeting is the launch uses my onboarding script: Add to Home Screen, PIN, pick your name, then a first Asante during the appreciation round.
2. **Push events: Modify.** At launch: (a) "X is going to [shop]", (b) "Someone thanked you", (c) gas or tokens hit **Out**. That matches Skeptic's maximum of 3. Help requests, money received, meeting day and Dad's summary move to 1.1, when the activity log can show whether they are needed. Quiet hours (21:00–07:00) are **non-negotiable** and cost little. Per-type mute shrinks to one on/off switch at launch. The "never send" list stays as policy.
3. **Thin Meals: Defer, with one exception.** Ingredients-to-shopping can wait for 1.1. **Copy last week stays in R1**: it is extra-small effort, and without it someone retypes 21 slots every Sunday, which is how a weekly feature dies in its second week.
4. **Navigation: Maintain Money on the bar, but swap Meals off.** I accept Pathfinder's five slots with More. The bar becomes **Home, Shop, Jobs, Money, More**, and More holds Meals, Meeting and Contacts. Today's cook already shows on Home.

**Reasoning:** People use the app daily for small taps, so friction and nagging decide whether it survives. Strategist's depth split matches how the house runs: stock, jobs and money are touched every day, and meals is a weekly planning moment that Home already surfaces. That is why Money earns the bar slot over Meals. Money is logged after every shop run and is the screen Dad opens. Hiding it under More makes the most sensitive data feel tucked away, which works against the "clear money" trust goal. On push, three useful events plus quiet hours beat seven events people learn to swipe away. The thank-you push matters because it is the only part of the volunteer model that rewards anyone, and the private, recipient-only nudge is what makes volunteering feel worth it without a scoreboard. Cutting it would leave only the transactional event and the alarm event, a system that asks without ever giving back.
