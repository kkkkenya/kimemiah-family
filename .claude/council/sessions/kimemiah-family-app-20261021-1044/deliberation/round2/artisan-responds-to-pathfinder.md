## Artisan Response to Pathfinder

**Their position:** A data-light, precached PWA shell. About 170 KB gzipped JS on first load, system fonts and SVG icons only, GSAP core for small transitions, Lenis with `syncTouch` off, at most one React Bits component (on an empty state), and a bottom bar of Home · Shop · Jobs · Meals · More with Money under More.

**My response:**

1. **Fonts: Modify.** I drop Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono and keep `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` for KES amounts. If Atkinson Next lacks tabular figures, amounts fall back to the system monospace. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next stays, self-hosted through `next/font/google` (Latin subset, weights 400 and 700, `display: swap`), precached by Serwist once and never fetched again. Young Serif stays as a single 400 weight, used only for section titles and the thank-you wall, with a Georgia / Noto Serif fallback. Font budget: **55 KB or less of woff2, counted separately from the 170 KB JS budget** and paid once at install. If Tuner's Slow-3G test fails, Young Serif goes first.
2. **React Bits: Modify.** CountUp goes. Dad's balance becomes a GSAP tween of about 10 lines (600ms, only when the value changed), which gives the same effect with no extra dependency. **SplitText stays as the single React Bits component.** It is loaded by `next/dynamic` after first paint and runs once a day on the Home greeting. That keeps the owner's mandatory React Bits rule on a screen people actually see, without blocking the tap path. If Pathfinder prefers, an empty state ("Nothing low. The house is stocked.") is an acceptable second location, not a replacement.
3. **Nav: Defer.** Five destinations with labels is the right density, and Money under More works if More remembers the last section opened. In exchange I ask that the Home "Money left" card be large and link straight to Money, so the balance is never more than one tap away for anyone. The active tab gets a jacaranda pill behind the icon, and every tab has a label, never an icon alone.

**Reasoning:** Pathfinder's budget is correct, and identity should not cost load time. The identity in my proposal mostly lives in places that cost nothing to load: the CSS kanga stripe, the colour tokens, and the hierarchy. Fonts are the one real cost. Cutting to roughly two files, precached once, keeps warmth in the thank-you wall while later opens stay close to 0 KB. System-only fonts would make the app look like any other Android form. That costs more than 50 KB here, because six people have to choose to keep using this every day. On motion we already agree: Lenis is limited to two read-only pages with `syncTouch` off, and GSAP core plus Flip stays within budget. Reducing React Bits to one lazy-loaded, once-a-day component meets the owner's rule and Pathfinder's limit together.
