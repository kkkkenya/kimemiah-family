# Household and goals

## Who lives here

Five adults:

| Person | Situation |
|---|---|
| Mom | Lives at home, runs much of the house today |
| Sister, 31 | Earning |
| Brother, 27 | In school |
| Brother, 25 | Trading forex, not yet profitable |
| Me | Not earning yet |

Dad lives elsewhere and sends money for shopping and the market.

## How things work today

- Dad's money covers supermarket shopping and the market.
- Responsibilities are not clearly shared. Some jobs fall on the same people; some get forgotten.
- There is no shared record of what's in the house, what's running out, or where the money went.

## What we want

1. **Freedom with collaboration.** Nobody is ordered around. People volunteer for jobs and help each other.
2. **Fairness people can see.** Open jobs, who picked them up, and thank-yous are visible to all, without scores or rankings.
3. **No surprises at home.** Gas, unga, cooking oil and tokens don't run out unexpectedly.
4. **Clear money.** Everyone, and Dad, can see what came in, what was spent and what's left.
5. **A regular family meeting** where we sort things out calmly and write down what we agreed.

## Principles for the app

- Updating must take seconds, or people will stop. One tap for most things.
- Appreciation over pressure. Thank-yous, no league tables.
- Not earning is not a reason to be left out. Time and effort count as contribution.
- Money records are factual and neutral: who, what, where, how much. No comments on people's spending in the app itself; that belongs in the meeting.
