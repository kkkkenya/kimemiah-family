Kimemiah Money, built the Monarch way

Monarch is a household money app: one place for spending, budgets, bills, goals and reports that a family shares. This is the MVP for bringing its best ideas into Kimemiah's Money and Jobs tabs, with Monarch's screen design on those tabs and on the money cards on Home. Stock and Shop keep their current look.

How it fits

What we take from Monarch, and what we change

M-Pesa instead of bank sync

Monarch connects to 13,000 banks. Kenyan family money moves through M-Pesa, cash and a few bank accounts, so entries come from a pasted M-Pesa SMS, a receipt photo, or a quick tap. Rules sort them automatically.

A house, not a couple

Monarch is built for two partners. Kimemiah has six people, and Peter funds most of it from away. Review, "who paid" and settling up work for any number of people, and the monthly summary goes to Peter on WhatsApp.

Money and Jobs move together

Monarch stops at money. Here a bill nearly due posts a job, a funded goal opens its job, and a question about a spend goes on Sunday's meeting agenda. People see what the house uses and do something about it.

Never ranked, never blamed

Usage notes talk about the house ("water 40% above usual"), not about a person. "Who paid" exists so money gets paid back, not to compare people.

2. Flex budgeting: one number to watch

Monarch's signature budget. Fixed costs are known, non-monthly costs are saved for separately, so the only number anyone needs to watch day to day is flexible money left. For the family that's food, market and airtime.

Monarch screen
A Fixed / Flexible / Non-monthly switch, a big ring for the flexible total, and the three groups listed under it with progress bars.
Kimemiah rules
Each kind of spending gets a group: Internet starts as Fixed, school fees and insurance as Non-monthly, everything else as Flexible. Non-monthly kinds roll over automatically. Plans repeat each month. Category budgeting stays available as one switch in Settings.
Forecast
After the 10th, each flexible kind says where it will end the month at the current pace.

4. Rules that sort M-Pesa for you

Monarch's rules engine is what keeps budgets accurate without effort. Our version learns from pasted M-Pesa messages: the first time a payee appears, a sheet offers to remember its kind, bill and shop.

Monarch screen
A bottom sheet: "If merchant is X → set category to Y", with a preview of how many past transactions match.
Kimemiah rules
Match on the M-Pesa payee, paybill or till number. Set kind, bill, shop. Applies when an SMS is pasted. Past entries stay as they are, since they can't be edited.

7. Settle up: what the house owes each person

A reviewer's favourite Monarch feature is staying up to date on what partners owe each other. In a family, people often pay a house cost from their own M-Pesa and forget to get it back. This makes it visible and easy to clear.

Monarch screen
A balance card per person with "owes" in the accent colour and a Settle button.
Kimemiah rules
A new "Paid from" field on money out: House money or "My own money". Own-money spends add to what the house owes that person. Paying them back is a money-out entry marked as a payback, which clears it.

Design

Monarch's look, on Money, Jobs and Home's money cards

Money, Jobs and the money cards on Home get a .monarch wrapper that swaps these tokens in. Stock and Shop keep the current look. Tailwind classes stay the same, and only the token values change inside the wrapper.

Linen canvas#efecea → --surface-base
Paper card#ffffff → --surface-raised
Ink#22201d → --text-primary
Graphite#777573 → --text-muted
Stone hairline#dcd9d6 → --line
Ember action#ff692d → --action, ink text

Type

Headings in Fraunces 350 with tight tracking (Monarch's Copernicus is paid). Body in Inter 400. Big money figures in the serif, with tabular digits.

Shape

Pill buttons and chips, 12px cards, 8px inputs. Cards get a hairline border and a 5% shadow, nothing heavier.

Accent

Ember only on the main button, the active tab, progress and eyebrow labels. Buttons use ink text on ember: white text on #ff692d is 2.9:1, too faint in sunlight.

Decided · 24 Oct 2026

What the family chose

Look
Monarch's look on Money, Jobs, and the money cards on Home (left this month, bills, goals). Stock and Shop keep the current look.
Budgeting
Flex budgeting from the start. Category budgeting stays one switch away in Settings.
Fixed costs
Internet. Everything else starts as Flexible; school fees and insurance go under Non-monthly when they're added.
Money in
Several people put money into the house. Each one is a source in the Sankey and in Peter's summary, and settle up stays in the MVP for costs paid from someone's own pocket.
